Environmental Sustainability in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is home to deserts, mountains, fertile valleys, coral reefs and a long coastline on the Red Sea and the Arabian Gulf. These natural resources support wildlife, communities and the economy, so protecting them is a responsibility shared by every student and citizen.

Why Protect Natural Resources?

Water

Water is scarce in the Kingdom. Saving it protects daily life and agriculture.

Energy

Using energy wisely cuts emissions and moves us towards cleaner power.

Biodiversity

Protecting wildlife and habitats keeps ecosystems healthy and balanced.

Alignment with Saudi Vision 2030

Saudi Vision 2030 sets a clear goal of a vibrant society, a thriving economy and an ambitious nation. Environmental protection is a core part of this vision, with a commitment to quality of life, sustainable development and the responsible use of resources.

The Saudi Green Initiative

The Saudi Green Initiative aims to reduce emissions, plant billions of trees, expand protected areas and increase the share of renewable energy. Together with the Middle East Green Initiative, it shows the Kingdom's regional and global leadership in climate action.
